# Third-party components

The RoboDojo control harness builds on the synchronous proposal, review and correction protocol and memory design from GPT-as-Policy. The corresponding upstream license is included in [licenses/GPT-as-Policy.txt](licenses/GPT-as-Policy.txt). [GPT-as-Policy source at the reference revision](https://github.com/anonymous-report-421/GPT-as-Policy/tree/8f3d362b077d8efb77e2a7274d5b2c20e2243846)

The vendored JustRUA observation modules provide measured gripper feedback. Their license and source revision accompany the code. [License](priora/robodojo/justrua_sensors/LICENSE) · [Provenance](priora/robodojo/justrua_sensors/PROVENANCE.md)

RoboDojo, Isaac Sim, Isaac Lab, cuRobo, Wall-X, WALL weights and Codex are external dependencies distributed by their respective providers. The bundled benchmark patch modifies RoboDojo task instruction text. The applicable upstream notice is included in [licenses/RoboDojo.txt](licenses/RoboDojo.txt).
